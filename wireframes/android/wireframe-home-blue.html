Design Notes

1 — Header

Personalized greeting with the user's name. Notification bell shows unread count (order updates, appointment reminders). Message icon links to direct messaging with staff.

2 — Search

Tapping opens a full-screen search with recent searches, category filters, and real-time product results. Searches frames, lenses, sunglasses, and accessories.

3 — Quick Actions

Four primary entry points: Shop (product catalog), Book Appt (appointment scheduling), AR Try-On (virtual eyewear try-on with camera), My Orders (order history and pickup status).

4 — Promo Banner

Swipeable carousel for promotions and new arrivals. Auto-rotates every 5 seconds. Tapping navigates to the relevant collection.

5 — Shop by Category

Horizontally scrollable category pills: Frames, Lenses, Contact Lenses, Sunglasses, Accessories. Tapping a pill filters the Featured Products below or navigates to a filtered catalog view. Active pill is highlighted in primary blue.

6 — Featured Products

Horizontally scrollable product cards now showing brand, name, star rating (1–5), and price. "AR" badge on items with virtual try-on. Star ratings are the average from customer feedbacks. Tapping opens the product detail page.

7 — Upcoming Appointment

Shows the next confirmed appointment at a glance. Tapping opens full details with reschedule/cancel options. Hidden when no upcoming appointments.

8 — Top Rated

Compact horizontal cards showing highest-rated products sorted by average rating. Each card displays thumbnail, name, star rating with review count (e.g. "5.0 (28)"), and price. Drives social proof and helps users discover the best items quickly.

9 — Customer Reviews

Horizontally scrollable review cards showing recent feedback. Each card includes: reviewer avatar + name, date, star rating, which product/service the review is for, and a comment excerpt. Reviews cover both products and services. "All Reviews" links to a dedicated reviews page. This builds trust and surfaces real customer experiences.

10 — Services

2x2 grid of available services: Eye Exam, Lens Fitting, Frame Adjustment, Warranty. Each card links to the booking flow pre-filled with that service type.

11 — Bottom Navigation

Five tabs: Home, Explore (full catalog with filters), Schedule (appointments), Orders (order history/pickup tracking), Profile (settings, submit feedback, logout).

12 — Explore

Shows a search bar, horizontally scrollable category filters, and a catalog grid of products that users can tap for details.

13 — Schedule

Displays appointment status lists (Upcoming/Past/Cancelled) with clear date/time at-a-glance and color-coded confirmation states.

14 — Orders

Provides order history with pickup status tags and a simple progress bar to communicate production or fulfillment stages.

15 — Profile & Settings

Includes a profile summary plus access to personal info, prescription history, notifications, privacy, and sign-out.

16 — Messages

Conversation list UI for messaging with doctors and support, including searchable threads and an unread count badge.

17 — Notifications

Grouped updates for key events (order ready, messages, reminders, and offers) presented as tappable rows.

18 — AR Try-On

A dedicated try-on view with a focused dark canvas, selectable frame styles, and a direct add-to-cart CTA.
